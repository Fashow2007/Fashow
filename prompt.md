# Fashion Career Marketplace — Website Design Brief

## 1. Website Overview

The website is a **two-sided platform connecting fashion companies with college students interested in beginning careers in fashion**.

Fashion companies can create profiles, post opportunities, internships, modeling opportunities, campaigns, events, and other jobs. Students can create profiles, discover opportunities, apply to positions, and build connections with fashion companies.

The primary goal is:

**Students get easier access to early-career fashion opportunities, while companies get direct access to young, motivated talent.**

The website should feel more like a **professional fashion networking platform** than a traditional job board.

---

# 2. User Types

There should be two primary account types.

### Student

Students use the platform to:

* Create a personal profile
* Add school, major, location, graduation year, interests, skills, and experience
* Upload photos/portfolio material
* Connect/link their LinkedIn
* Discover opportunities
* Search and filter opportunities
* Save opportunities
* Apply to opportunities
* Track applications
* Message companies
* Receive notifications
* View companies and their profiles

### Fashion Company

Companies use the platform to:

* Create a company profile
* Describe their brand
* Upload company/brand images
* List their location and website
* Create opportunity posts
* Manage applications
* Search/discover students
* Contact students
* Post new positions or opportunities
* Edit/remove existing posts
* View applicants

# 24. MVP — What Should Actually Be Built First

For the first version, I would **not** try to build every feature above.

The MVP should focus on the core interaction:

**Student → discovers opportunity → applies → company reviews applicant**

So the first version should contain:

1. Landing page
2. Student registration/login
3. Company registration/login
4. Student profile
5. Company profile
6. Opportunity feed
7. Search/filter
8. Opportunity detail page
9. Post opportunity
10. Apply to opportunity
11. Student application tracking
12. Company applicant management

Messaging, recommendations, analytics, advanced LinkedIn integration, etc. can come afterward.

# 25. The Core Product Concept

The most important thing for the freelancer to understand is that this **isn't simply "Indeed for fashion."**

The product should combine three things:

**Career discovery**

Students find opportunities they otherwise may never encounter.

**Professional networking**

Students build relationships directly with companies.

**Fashion portfolio**

Students can showcase themselves visually rather than only submitting a traditional resume.

That third piece is what should heavily influence the UI. A fashion student should be able to open the site and immediately feel like they are entering a **fashion career/networking environment**, rather than a generic employment portal.
