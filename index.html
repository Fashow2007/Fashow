Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Fashow Technologies Inc. ("Fashow", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information across our fashion career marketplace.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only necessary information required to facilitate career opportunities:

  • Student profile data (name, university, major, graduation year, portfolio links)
  • Application materials submitted directly by users (resumes, lookbook URLs, cover notes)
  • Company business information (business email, brand name, verified website)

2. Purpose of Processing

Information is used exclusively to connect students with employers, manage job applications, and prevent platform misuse. We never sell your personal data to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to inspect, download, or permanently delete your student profile and application history at any time by contacting our privacy compliance officer at sales@gennis.shop.

Terms & Conditions

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Platform Nature

Fashow serves as a direct matchmaking venue connecting emerging creative talent with verified fashion brands. Fashow is not an employer, employment agency, or party to individual employment contracts formed between students and brands.

2. Intellectual Property of Student Portfolios

Students retain 100% full ownership, copyright, and moral rights to all creative works, photographs, designs, and portfolio material shared on the platform. Brands may only review portfolio assets for hiring and casting evaluation.

3. Fair Labor Compliance

All posted positions must comply with federal, state, and local fair labor standards. Compensation transparency is strictly enforced across all listings.

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

We use essential browser cookies and local storage tokens solely to maintain your login session, save your bookmarked opportunities, and preserve your cookie preferences.

We do not deploy cross-site tracking beacons or invasive commercial ad tracking cookies.

Refund & Billing Policy

Students: Fashow is 100% free for college students and emerging candidates.

Companies: For paid enterprise job posting packages, cancellations requested within 14 days of purchase with no active candidate contact will receive a full refund. Contact sales@gennis.shop for billing inquiries.

We use essential cookies and local storage to provide authentic session management and save your opportunity bookmarks. Read our Cookies Policy for details.
