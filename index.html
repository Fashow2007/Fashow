ABOUT FASHOW

A fashion talent and creator network built around college culture.

Fashow is not simply another influencer marketing dashboard or UGC platform. We believe the future of fashion is being shaped in college communities—on university walkways, in dorm room studios, on student runways, and in independent collegiate design collectives.

Our mission is to give emerging college models, creators, photographers and stylists direct access to verified fashion houses, without gatekeepers or agency commissions.

Starting in Amherst, Massachusetts, Fashow is designed from day one to expand across major college markets, fashion capitals, and eventually globally.
